# 🎮 Hytale Modded

Welcome to **Hytale Modded** - A community-driven organization dedicated to creating and maintaining high-quality mods for Hytale!

## 👋 About Us

Hytale Modded is an organization founded by [xCykrix](https://github.com/xCykrix) to house and develop innovative modifications for Hytale. Our mission is to enhance the Hytale experience by providing powerful, server-side mods that add new features and capabilities to the game.

## 🚀 Our Projects

### [Announcer](https://github.com/Hytale-Modded/Announcer)

A server-side Hytale mod that enables powerful announcement capabilities for server administrators, including custom titles, subtitles, and MOTD on player join.
