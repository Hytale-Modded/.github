# 🎮 Hytale Modded

Welcome to **Hytale Modded** - A community-driven organization dedicated to creating and maintaining high-quality mods for Hytale!

## 👋 About Us

Hytale Modded is an organization founded by [xCykrix](https://github.com/xCykrix) to house and develop innovative modifications for Hytale. Our mission is to enhance the Hytale experience by providing powerful, server-side mods that add new features and capabilities to the game.

## 🚀 Our Projects

### [Hytale-Announcer](https://github.com/xCykrix/Hytale-Announcer)

A server-side Hytale mod that enables powerful announcement capabilities for server administrators.

**Features:**
- 📢 Send general announcements to all players
- 🎯 Display custom titles and subtitles
- 📝 Show MOTD (Message of the Day) on player join
- ⚙️ Configurable messages and settings
- 🛠️ Simple command-based interface

**Quick Start:**
```bash
/title "Title Text" "Subtitle Text" 10 5 5
/announce "Your announcement message"
```
